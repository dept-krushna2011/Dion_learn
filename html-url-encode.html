🔗 HTML URL Encode

URL encoding converts characters into a format that can be safely transmitted over the internet. URLs can only contain ASCII characters — anything else must be encoded.

🧠 Why it matters: URLs cannot have spaces, special characters, or non-ASCII characters (like é, ñ, 中文). URL encoding replaces unsafe characters with a % followed by two hexadecimal digits.

⚙️ How URL Encoding Works

URL encoding replaces unsafe characters with %XX where XX is the character's hexadecimal code:

CharacterURL EncodedReason
Space%20 or +URLs cannot have spaces
!%21Special character
#%23Reserved for anchors
$%24Special character
%%25Reserved for encoding
&%26Reserved for parameters
@%40Special character
/%2FReserved for path

📝 URL Encoding Examples

Original URL:
https://example.com/search?q=hello world!

Encoded URL:
https://example.com/search?q=hello%20world%21

Another example:
Original: https://site.com/ünîcødé
Encoded:  https://site.com/%C3%BCn%C3%AEc%C3%B8d%C3%A9
