🎬 HTML Media

HTML makes it super easy to add videos, audio, YouTube videos, and plug-ins to your web pages. No special software needed — just simple HTML tags!

🧠 In Simple Words: Just like you use <img> to show pictures, you use <video> to show videos and <audio> to play sounds. It's that easy!

📺 What is Multimedia?

Multimedia = different types of content combined together. On websites, multimedia means:

TypeFile ExtensionsHTML Tag
🎥 Video.mp4, .webm, .ogg<video>
🔊 Audio.mp3, .wav, .ogg<audio>
▶️ YouTube VideosEmbedded link<iframe>
🧩 Plug-insFlash, Java (old tech)<object> / <embed>

🎥 Video — Quick Preview

Adding a video is as simple as this:

<video width="320" controls>
  <source src="movie.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  Your browser does not support video.
</video>

💡 The controls attribute adds play, pause, volume buttons automatically. Without it, users can't control the video!

🔊 Audio — Quick Preview

Adding audio works the same way:

<audio controls>
  <source src="song.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
  Your browser does not support audio.
</audio>

▶️ YouTube Videos — Quick Preview

To add a YouTube video, just copy the embed code from any YouTube video:

<iframe width="560" height="315" 
  src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/VIDEO_ID">
</iframe>

That's it! No special software, no plugins — just copy and paste!
